# Fast Food Sales Analysis

### From transactional data to business insights through quantitative analysis and interactive visualization

This project explores the sales performance of a fictional fast-food company, combining **Microsoft Excel** for data preparation and quantitative analysis with **Tableau** for interactive data visualization.

Rather than focusing only on dashboard creation, the project follows a structured analytical workflow: starting from raw transactional data, transforming it into measurable indicators, comparing performance across different dimensions, and presenting the results through an interactive business dashboard.

My background in **Mathematics** influenced the analytical approach adopted throughout the project, particularly in the use of descriptive statistics, quantitative comparisons and structured interpretation of numerical results.

---

## Analytical Objectives

The analysis was designed around a set of business-oriented questions:

* How much revenue was generated during the observed period?
* How many units were sold?
* Which products contributed most to revenue and sales volume?
* How does revenue vary across managers?
* Which payment methods generated the highest revenue?
* How is revenue distributed across purchase channels?
* How did sales performance change between November and December 2022?
* How do these indicators change when analysing individual managers?

The objective was not only to calculate these metrics, but to create an analytical environment in which they could be explored dynamically.

---

## Quantitative Approach

The mathematical component of the project is reflected in the transformation of transactional records into interpretable quantitative indicators.

The analysis included:

* calculation and comparison of aggregate measures;
* descriptive statistical analysis of numerical variables;
* analysis of revenue and sales-volume distributions;
* comparison of performance across categorical dimensions;
* temporal comparison of sales results;
* manager-level performance analysis;
* interpretation of central values and aggregated business metrics.

This approach allowed individual transactions to be translated into a structured view of company performance.

---

## Data Preparation & Excel Analysis

Microsoft Excel was used as the first analytical environment.

The workflow included:

**Data preparation**

* inspection of the original dataset;

**Exploratory and quantitative analysis**

* Pivot Tables;
* descriptive statistics;
* revenue aggregation;
* quantity analysis;
* product-level comparisons;
* manager-level comparisons;

* purchase-location analysis;
* monthly performance analysis.

The Excel workbook included in this repository documents the analytical process behind the final dashboard.

---

## Interactive Tableau Dashboard

The results were translated into an interactive **Tableau dashboard** designed to make the main business indicators immediately accessible while still allowing deeper exploration.

![Sales Performance Dashboard](dashboard_preview.png)

The dashboard includes:

* **Total Revenue**
* **Total Units Sold**
* **Revenue by Product**
* **Product Sales Volume**
* **Revenue by Manager**
* **Revenue by Payment Method**
* **Revenue by Purchase Location**
* **Monthly Sales Trend**

Filters allow the analysis to be dynamically segmented by **manager** and **time period**, with KPIs and visualizations updating according to the selected context.

## Key Results

Across the complete dataset:

**Total Revenue:** €812,135
**Total Units Sold:** 118,539

Beyond these overall KPIs, the dashboard makes it possible to investigate how performance changes across products, managers, payment methods, purchase channels and time periods.

The purpose of the analysis is therefore not limited to reporting a single result: it provides a framework for **comparative and multidimensional exploration of sales performance**.
